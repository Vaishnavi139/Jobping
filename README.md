# JobPing

**Be the first to apply.** A personal job-search assistant that watches new postings for you and pings you the moment a great match appears.

Tell it the roles you want — titles, experience, location, work mode. It scores every new posting against your resume, ATS-style, and emails you only about the ones worth your time. No doomscrolling job boards, no missed postings. You always apply yourself.

## Why I built it

I built JobPing for my own job search. I was tired of refreshing job boards every day and watching good postings fill up before I'd even seen them. I wanted the matches to come to me — by email — so I could be among the first to apply.
