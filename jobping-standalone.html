Get matching jobs in your inbox — takes about 30 seconds:

  1. Tap Get your free key — it opens web3forms.com in a new tab.
  2. On their site, type your email and submit. They'll show you an access key.
  3. Copy that key, come back here, paste it in the box below, and tap Save key.
  4. Tap Send test email and check your inbox. That's it — you'll now get digests automatically.
